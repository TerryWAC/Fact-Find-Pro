# FactFind Pro — classroom launch copy

Prepared 13 September 2026 for the current application. This replaces the older classroom description; it has not been posted to Skool.

## FactFind Pro

FactFind Pro gives advisers four branded online forms: Mortgage & Protection, Protection, Medical and Home Insurance. Clients complete a web link on their phone, tablet or computer. Completed records appear in your adviser dashboard, ready to review and download as a PDF.

### Make it yours

Add your company name, logo, brand colour and optional adviser photo. Your forms and client-copy emails carry your branding. Emails are sent from the FactFind platform domain; replies to client copies go to you.

### Four forms, one workspace

- **Mortgage & Protection:** client details, income and expenditure, borrowing and credit history, property requirements and protection needs.
- **Protection:** dependants, income, bills, sick pay, savings and existing life, critical illness and income protection cover.
- **Medical:** health, lifestyle and family history, with the original disclosure and accuracy questions.
- **Home Insurance:** property details, buildings and contents requirements, security and claims history.

Questions follow the existing Typeform routes, showing relevant sections based on previous answers. Mortgage & Protection and Protection include the original adviser/client choice and adviser-only notes. Medical and Home can also be completed during an adviser meeting, but do not contain a separate adviser/client selector or internal-notes section.

### What happens when a form is completed

Clients review their answers before submitting. The completed record appears in your dashboard with a reference, submission date and review status. Download a branded PDF, or send the client their PDF from the submission page. Internal adviser notes are excluded from client copies.

Choose your automatic email preferences in Settings: an adviser PDF, a client PDF, both or neither. Your preferences also apply when you complete a form during a meeting.

Records remain in the platform until deletion is requested and processed. Archiving is a workflow status and does not delete a record. You remain responsible for reviewing whether your records are still needed and for meeting your firm's record-keeping obligations.

### Get started

1. Register your practice and read the linked terms of use and privacy notice.
2. Wait for the Wealthy Advisers Club team to review your registration. We will email you when approved.
3. Sign in and complete your details, branding and PDF email preferences. You can finish optional setup details later in Settings.
4. Open your dashboard and copy your four unique client links. They are created automatically on approval.
5. Test your links, then use them in appointment emails, WhatsApp messages or your existing CRM workflow.

Example message: “Ahead of our call, please complete this fact find so I can prepare for our conversation. Keep the tab open until you submit, and check your answers on the review screen.”

### Using a phone or tablet

Open the same link on desktop, tablet or mobile. You can add the website to your home screen for quick access. Clients do not need an account or an app download.

### Before you start a form

Keep the form tab open until you submit. Answers are currently saved on submission; unfinished drafts, partial-submission tracking and cross-device resume are not available. An internet connection is required.

Completed records are stored in your adviser dashboard, rather than automatically copied into a private spreadsheet. A colleague-directory entry does not create a shared login or grant access to client records.
